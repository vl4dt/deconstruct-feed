Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2, 2025

Introduction

Deconstruct Feed ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome Extension ("Extension") handles your data.

Summary: We do not collect, store, or sell your personal data. All AI analysis happens locally on your device or via your own API keys.

Data Collection and Usage

1. Personal Information

We do not collect any personal information (such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers) through the Extension.

2. Browsing History

The Extension only accesses the content of the specific web pages you choose to analyze. This data is processed in your browser's memory and is not sent to our servers.

3. AI Analysis Data

  • Chrome Built-in AI: All processing happens locally on your device. No data leaves your computer.
  • External AI Providers: If you use Gemini, OpenAI, or Anthropic, your article content is sent directly from your browser to their APIs using your keys. We do not see this data.

4. API Keys

Your API keys are stored locally in your browser's secure storage (chrome.storage.local). They are never sent to our servers.

Third-Party Services

We use Paddle.com for payment processing. If you purchase a Pro license, your payment information is handled entirely by Paddle. We do not store your credit card details.
