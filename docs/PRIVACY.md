# Privacy Policy for Deconstruct Feed

**Last Updated: December 2, 2025**

## Introduction

Deconstruct Feed ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome Extension ("Extension") handles your data.

**Summary: We do not collect, store, or sell your personal data. All AI analysis happens locally on your device or via your own API keys.**

## Data Collection and Usage

### 1. Personal Information
We do not collect any personal information (such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers) through the Extension.

### 2. Browsing History
The Extension only accesses the content of the specific web pages you choose to analyze. This data is processed in your browser's memory and is not sent to our servers.

### 3. AI Analysis Data
- **Chrome Built-in AI**: All processing happens locally on your device. No data leaves your computer.
- **External AI Providers (Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic)**: If you choose to use these providers, your article content is sent directly from your browser to their respective APIs using your own API keys. We do not act as a middleman and do not see or store this data.
- **Local LLM**: If you use a local LLM (e.g., Ollama), all data remains on your local network.

### 4. API Keys
Your API keys (for Gemini, OpenAI, etc.) are stored locally in your browser's secure storage (`chrome.storage.local`). They are never sent to our servers.

## Third-Party Services

The Extension may interact with the following third-party services if you configure them:
- **Google Gemini API**: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **OpenAI API**: [OpenAI Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/privacy)
- **Anthropic API**: [Anthropic Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/privacy)
- **Paddle**: We use Paddle for payment processing. If you purchase a Pro license, your payment information is handled entirely by Paddle. We do not store your credit card details. [Paddle Privacy Policy](https://www.paddle.com/legal/privacy)

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
